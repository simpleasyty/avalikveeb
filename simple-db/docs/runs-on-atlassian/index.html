TRUST & SECURITY

Runs on Atlassian — Simple DB

Understand how Simple DB uses Atlassian Forge for compute and storage, and what to review before installation.

What is Runs on Atlassian?

Runs on Atlassian (ROA) is a trust badge for eligible Forge apps in the Atlassian Marketplace. It gives customers a quick way to identify apps whose compute and storage stay on Atlassian infrastructure and that meet Atlassian's requirements for data residency and controlled data egress.

A simpler way to understand where your app data goes.

Review these three boundaries when assessing the app for your team.

  • Hosted by AtlassianForge provides the app’s compute and hosted storage. No separate Simpleasyty database is needed for its core data.→
  • Data residencyIn-scope Forge-hosted data follows supported Atlassian residency controls. Check the site and app status in Atlassian administration.→
  • Controlled data egressReview declared external analytics and your organization’s controls separately from database storage.→

What the badge means in practice

Simple DB runs on Atlassian Forge. Forge SQL stores relational app data and Forge KVS stores supporting records. Simpleasyty develops and supports the app; Atlassian provides the hosting platform. The badge is not an Atlassian endorsement or a substitute for reviewing permissions and privacy.

Usage analytics and database content

The app declares limited external usage analytics separately from its Forge database storage. Review the privacy policy and permissions for your organization’s requirements; Forge hosting does not mean that all network traffic is disabled.
