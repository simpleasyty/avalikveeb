Compliance-ready by design

Turn every compliance requirement into a structured, reviewable record.

Simple Metadata keeps the evidence, owner, status, scope and review date on the Confluence page where the work happens. Schemas define what is required; validation and history make the result easier to review and explain.

◎

One source of truth

Define required fields, types, constraints, defaults and eligible spaces once. Every capture form and report follows the same schema.

✓

Evidence you can test

Validation shows whether an entry matches its schema. Indexed fields make gaps, owners, due dates and review states visible in reports.

↺

A record of change

Entry history and schema revisions preserve the context behind a decision, while permissions keep governance scoped to the right spaces and groups.

Templates are the starting point

Start with the control record your team actually needs.

A template turns a framework requirement into a repeatable schema. Add the fields your reviewers need, assign responsibility, capture the evidence on the page, and report on what still needs attention.

Control and policy record

Control ID, scope, owner, evidence link, review date, status and approval.

Vendor due diligence

Supplier, risk tier, contract date, assessor, findings and remediation owner.

Access and review

System, reviewer, access decision, exception, expiry and next review.

Certificate evidence pack

Collect the metadata needed to assemble an auditable pack for a framework or certification review.

A practical compliance workflow

From template to evidence pack, without creating another system of record.

01

Choose a template

Start with a provided schema or adapt one to your framework.

02

Capture evidence

Collect structured data in the Confluence pages your teams already use.

03

Review the gaps

Filter indexed fields, inspect validation state and follow revisions.

04

Prepare the pack

Export schema and evidence data for your internal or external review process.

Simple Metadata helps teams organise and evidence their controls. It does not issue an official certificate or replace an accredited assessor.
