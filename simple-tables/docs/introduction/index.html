Introduction

Simple Tables lets you turn CSV, JSON, or Excel files (.xls/.xlsx), existing Confluence tables, and live Confluence content into interactive, in-page tables. It runs on Atlassian with no data egress as part of its core functionality; customer data is not transmitted or stored outside Atlassian’s infrastructure.

Start by importing data, enhancing a native Confluence table, or building a live report from Confluence Content and Page Properties. Whether you're working with financial reports, customer logs, or operational metrics, Simple Tables makes your data readable and actionable within seconds. Rich formatting, column types, and footer aggregations help teams analyze faster—without needing to export or reformat anything.

We continue expanding Simple Tables with new controls and calculations each quarter. If your specific workflow or use-case isn’t covered, reach out to our team—your feedback drives our roadmap.

App interface languages

The Simple Tables app interface follows the Confluence language where it is available in English, German, or Japanese. Macro titles, setup controls, filters, and app-page controls use the matching language; other Confluence languages fall back to English.

This applies to the in-product interface only. It does not translate your table data, formulas, column names, or this documentation website.
