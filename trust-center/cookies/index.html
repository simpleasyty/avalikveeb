How we use cookies on our website

This Cookie Policy explains how Simpleasyty uses cookies and similar technologies on our public website (not in our apps).

We use a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics solution (Umami) to understand how visitors interact with our website and improve the browsing experience. This analytics setup does not rely on tracking cookies.

The data collected is anonymized and aggregated. It does not include personally identifiable information (PII), and it is not used to identify individual visitors or track them across websites.

In some cases, basic technical cookies may be used by the website or hosting infrastructure to ensure proper functionality. These cookies are essential and are not used for marketing or profiling purposes.

Simpleasyty apps do not use cookies or store tracking data inside the Atlassian environment. This policy applies exclusively to the public website (simpleasyty.com).

For more details on how we handle data, see ourPrivacy Policy.
