Security and infrastructure

At Simpleasyty, we take a security-first approach. Our apps are built for Atlassian Forge and are designed to fit inside the Atlassian environment customers already use and manage.

COMPLIANCE

SECURITY AT A GLANCE

Security that stays close to the platform

A clear, platform-native approach helps customers understand where the product runs, what it accesses, and how to review it.

Built for Atlassian

Core app functionality runs within Atlassian-managed infrastructure.

Data kept to a minimum

The product is not designed around separate customer data storage outside Atlassian.

Access with a purpose

We ask only for the access needed to deliver a clear user-facing feature.

How core app data is handled

For core app functionality, customer data is processed within Atlassian-managed infrastructure. We do not position the app around separate customer data storage outside Atlassian.

Simpleasyty apps do not independently store or process personally identifiable information (PII) outside of Atlassian’s infrastructure, and we only ask for the access needed to deliver the product’s intended behavior.

The same platform-native approach is what we mean by “runs on Atlassian”: the product stays close to the platform, rather than relying on an additional vendor-hosted application layer.

For the public website, we use separate privacy-friendly analytics. That is distinct from the app’s core data path.

Permission philosophy

We keep permissions as narrow as the product allows and try not to ask for access that does not support a clear user-facing feature.

Incident response

If customers need help, the public support page is the first stop. If an issue looks platform-wide, Atlassian status is the relevant source. When we ship a fix or product update, the changelog is where customers can verify it.
